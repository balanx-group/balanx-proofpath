Public benefit, observable limits

Make careful behavior
easier to inspect.

Errors in everyday administrative workflows can waste time, expose unnecessary information, or send the same request twice. These fictional cases make those failure modes repeatable without involving real applicants.

What this prototype demonstrates

  • Authority can change. A newer consent or policy record can invalidate a saved plan.
  • Receipts belong to an intent. A nearby record is not proof that this request succeeded.
  • Recovery needs evidence. An interrupted workflow should reconcile its outcome before trying again.
  • Less disclosure is a measurable choice. Only authorized, needed fields belong in the next step.

Proposed integration · not connected

A future assistant should carry
the user’s intent—not extra authority.

BalanX proposes exploring account continuity for its beta products and research workflows. Where approved and eligible, users could choose identity alone or supported use of an eligible service plan. This prototype does not implement authentication, connect an account, or make AI requests.

A research integration could evaluate whether an assistant preserves the intended task through interruptions and changing evidence, then compare its decisions with reproducible checks. Any real integration would require the relevant approval, documented permissions, and testing beyond these open synthetic examples.
